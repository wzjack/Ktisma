KTISMA CAPITAL GROUP - BRAND KIT
================================
Final logo: the sliced K and TISMA are the same height and sit on one baseline, with CAPITAL GROUP centered underneath
between two rules. Everything here is true vector artwork, so it stays razor sharp at any size.

USAGE NOTES
- Primary logo on ivory or white. Reversed logo on navy or dark photography. One-color versions when only one ink is available.
- The files include a margin of about a fifth of the logo's height. Keep that space clear of other elements.
- Minimum sizes: full logo about 36 mm (1.4 in) or 180 px wide. Below that, use the simplified wordmark or the K monogram.
- The K alone is the icon: favicon, social profile picture, app tiles. The favicon sizes use a bolder K with wider gaps
  between the courses, so the K stays readable at 16 to 48 px.
- The business card back carries only the monogram. Send your name, title and contact details to add the text.
- Say it "k-TIZ-muh". Spell the name out in words on the About page, since a logo cannot fix pronunciation.

GOOD TO KNOW
- The TISMA and CAPITAL GROUP lettering was traced from your uploaded image, so it matches that design exactly, but it is
  outlines, not a font. TISMA is enlarged 35% in the final logo. The K is rebuilt as clean geometry. The plain K in the
  simplified wordmark was traced from your sheet.
- For very large signage (several meters wide), have a type designer do a final redraw of the lettering.
